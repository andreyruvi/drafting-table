This page is example content. Edit `about.md` to introduce your studio: what you
do, the software you work in, the deliverables you hand over, and how to reach
you.

## Editing the theme's details

Studio name, role line, location and email come from `_config.yml`:

```yaml
studio:
  name: Your Studio
  role: Building design and BIM
  location: Your city
  email: you@example.com
```

The area unit is set there too. Switch it to `sq ft` and every area on every
project page follows:

```yaml
units:
  area: "sq ft"
```
